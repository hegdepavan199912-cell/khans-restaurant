WELCOME TO KHAN’S

A London Tradition

Experience authentic Indian cuisine at Khan’s Restaurant, where traditional flavours, aromatic spices and carefully prepared dishes come together. Established in 1977, Khan’s brings generations of Indian culinary tradition to Bayswater, London.
